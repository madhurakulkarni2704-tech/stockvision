 StockVision - Project Scope

 1. Introduction

StockVision is a web-based inventory management system designed to help small retailers manage their products, stock levels, sales, and expiry information in one centralized platform.

The project mainly focuses on perishable products where expiry and product wastage are important concerns.

 2. Problem Statement

Small retailers often manage inventory using notebooks, spreadsheets, or basic systems. This can make it difficult to maintain accurate stock information and monitor products with limited shelf life.

Common problems include:

* Difficulty tracking available stock
* Missed product expiry dates
* Overstocking
* Low-stock situations
* Product wastage
* Financial losses
* Difficulty deciding suitable discounts for products nearing expiry

StockVision aims to address these problems through a centralized digital inventory management system.

3. Project Objectives

The main objectives of StockVision are:

1. To provide digital inventory management for small retailers.
2. To maintain product and stock information.
3. To monitor product expiry dates.
4. To identify products that are nearing expiry.
5. To provide alerts for important inventory conditions.
6. To recommend suitable discounts for products nearing expiry.
7. To record and monitor product sales.
8. To provide dashboards and reports for better decision-making.
9. To help reduce product wastage and inventory losses.

4. Scope of the Project

The project will cover the following major areas.

 4.1 User Management

* User registration
* User login
* Logout
* Role-based access

 4.2 Product Management

* Add products
* View products
* Update products
* Delete products
* Manage product categories
* Store product expiry information

 4.3 Inventory Management

* Track available stock
* Monitor low-stock products
* Identify out-of-stock products
* Update stock based on sales

 4.4 Expiry Monitoring

The system will calculate the remaining shelf life of products and classify products according to their expiry status.

Possible statuses include:

* Safe
* Warning
* Urgent
* Expired

 4.5 Smart Alerts

The system will provide alerts for conditions such as:

* Low stock
* Products nearing expiry
* Expired products

 4.6 Dynamic Pricing

StockVision will provide discount recommendations for selected products based on factors such as:

* Days remaining until expiry
* Current stock quantity
* Product information

The recommendation system will help retailers make timely pricing decisions for products nearing expiry.

 4.7 Sales Management

The system will allow retailers to:

* Record sales
* Track sold quantities
* Maintain sales history
* Update inventory after sales

 4.8 Dashboard and Reports

The system will provide information such as:

* Total products
* Current inventory
* Low-stock products
* Products nearing expiry
* Expired products
* Sales information
* Discount recommendations

 5. Target Users

 Retailer / Shopkeeper

The primary user who will manage:

* Products
* Inventory
* Sales
* Expiry information
* Alerts
* Discount recommendations
* Reports

 Administrator

The administrator will manage:

* Users
* Product categories
* System-level information
* Overall system monitoring

 6. Out of Scope

The initial version of StockVision will not focus on:

* Online payment processing
* Delivery management
* Complete e-commerce functionality
* Supplier payment processing
* Physical barcode scanner integration
* Advanced AI-based demand forecasting

These features may be considered as future enhancements.

 7. Expected Benefits

StockVision is expected to help retailers:

* Save time in inventory management.
* Maintain organized product records.
* Identify products nearing expiry.
* Reduce avoidable product wastage.
* Make better discount decisions.
* Monitor stock and sales more efficiently.

 8. Future Enhancements

Possible future enhancements include:

* AI-based demand prediction
* Barcode and QR code integration
* Mobile application
* Advanced sales forecasting
* Automated notifications
* Supplier management
* Advanced business analytics
